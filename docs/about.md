## Authors

The present computational implementations and numerical validations accompany the manuscript: **"Power Generation in a Silica Microchannel Driven by Forward Osmosis"**, which was recently submitted to the journal *Tecnología y Ciencia Aplicada* (ISSN 2992-8338).
